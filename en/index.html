The Miral Story

Where Culinary Passion Meets an Illuminated White Canvas

«Miral» was born from a visionary ambition to reinvent fine dining in Riyadh: departing from shadowy dining rooms and cultivating an illuminated, vibrant sanctuary radiating life and architectural grace.

Every dish presented at Miral is a harmonized masterpiece. The voyage commences from single-estate cold-pressed olive oils, through line-caught Red Sea delicacies, down to slow cooking over aged olive wood.

"Cooking is not merely executing recipes; it is an unspoken dialogue between the chef's spirit and the guest's senses. At Miral, each bite is crafted to become a lasting memory."

Chef Fabrizio Laurent • Executive Head Chef
