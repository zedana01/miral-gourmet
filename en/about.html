The Miral Vision

An Illuminated Canvas and Culinary Craft Without Boundaries

Miral Gourmet embarked from a bold vision: to redefine luxury dining in the heart of Riyadh by breaking away from dark, heavy dining rooms, introducing instead an airy architectural haven bathed in natural light and vibrant beauty.

We hold that every dish served is an integrated art piece; starting with artisanal cold-pressed olive oils, through Red Sea fish caught daily at dawn, down to meticulous slow-cooking techniques that impart memorable depth to every flavor.

"We do not merely cook dishes; we safeguard the identity of fine dining and weave memories that endure."

Chef Fabrizio Laurent • Executive Head Chef
